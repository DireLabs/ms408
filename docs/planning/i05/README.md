# MS408 Research Program — Iteration 05 (the mid-level linguistic program)

**Mandate (L37).** i01–i04 established that Voynichese is a genuine, richly structured,
two-system script but that *magnitude*-of-structure statistics cannot bridge to
meaning (E9: they are structure detectors, not meaning detectors). i05 builds the
**missing middle** — the *kind*-of-structure layers that sit between raw structure and
semantics, the way a human learns a language: **morphology → word-classes → syntax**,
before any vocabulary/meaning. The goal is to characterise the GRAMMAR of the system
and, in doing so, to reweight (not resolve) the meaningful-vs-meaningless question.

Two of Tim's hypotheses drive it:
1. **A vs B may be two different generative processes** ("one gibberish, one
   language"), and pooling them seeds the decoding difficulty. So **Currier A/B is the
   primary stratification of every i05 probe**, and the A-vs-B contrast is a
   first-class deliverable — not "are they different" (known, F2) but "do they have the
   same MID-LEVEL grammar (word-classes, function/content split, morphology)".
2. **We have jumped structure→meaning too aggressively.** The mid-level layers are the
   grounded, defensible path: they can prove "language-like grammar to degree X"
   without ever claiming a translation.

**Inheritance.** All locks L1–L37; standing refutation rule; firewall (L3); evidence
grading (L6). Continues the experiment ledger as **E13–E16**.

## Standing requirements specific to i05

- **Kind, not magnitude (the E9 lesson).** Every probe asks *what kind* of structure,
  not how much. A probe that only measures "amount of structure" is not admissible —
  E9 showed those cannot separate meaning.
- **Harness-first, extended (L4).** Each probe is calibrated on H4 **real languages**
  (Latin, German, Italian, Hebrew) AND on the **null/generator controls** (H3 self-
  citation, conlang, drift, abjad) BEFORE it is interpreted on the manuscript. We must
  know what "natural-language grammar" and "generator grammar" each look like on the
  probe; only then does a VMS value mean anything. State the calibrated decision rule
  before running on the VMS.
- **A/B primary stratification (L8).** Every probe runs on VMS-A and VMS-B separately
  and is compared; also a v101 sensitivity pass (L11).
- **The L7 ceiling (reaffirmed).** A validated grammar characterises the system and
  reweights meaningful-vs-meaningless; it does NOT license translation without an
  independent semantic anchor. The generators HAVE grammar without meaning — so
  "rich grammar" raises the weight on *language* but never proves *meaning*.
- **Refutation pass** on every result before A/B grading.

## Success criterion (i05)

For each mid-level probe, a graded verdict — calibrated against real-language and
generator controls, stratified by A/B — that survives a fresh refutation pass, plus a
consolidated **A-vs-B grammar contrast**: do the two Currier systems share a mid-level
grammar, or do they behave like different generative processes? Decipherment remains
out of scope (L1–L7).
