# Publication planning — H2 2026 (→ early Q1 2027)

Planning hub for getting the program's two papers into the research literature in the next
~6 months. Outbound-research driven; venue facts carry source URLs and confidence flags.

## The two papers

- **Paper A — "constraint-envelope"** (`paper/v7`): firewall-disciplined computational study
  of MS 408 that *shrinks the hypothesis space*; every claim evidence-graded; **no decipherment
  claim**. Audience: computational linguistics / digital humanities / manuscript studies.
- **Paper B — "methods"** (`paper/methods/v3`): transferable methodology — adversarial
  self-correction (harness + firewall + clean-context refutation) for ground-truth-free
  corpora; Voynich is the worked example. Audience: NLP methods / reproducibility / DH.

## The single most important finding

**Every relevant venue rejects a perceived "Voynich solution" and welcomes
hypothesis-shrinking / negative-results / methodology framings.** That is exactly our posture
(L7: no decipherment). Our firewall discipline is an asset, not a liability, at these venues.
