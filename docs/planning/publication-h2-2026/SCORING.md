# Opportunity scoring

A rubric to score each (paper × venue) opportunity, so pathway choice is deliberate, not
vibes. Scores are **reasoned judgments (grade C)** from the venue research — refine as facts
firm up (e.g. unconfirmed deadlines, aggregator APCs). Each criterion 1–5 (5 = best), weighted.

## Criteria & weights

| # | Criterion | Weight | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scope fit** | 0.25 | paper is squarely in-scope | tangential |
| 2 | **Precedent / de-risk** | 0.15 | venue has published Voynich/undeciphered/adjacent work | none; unknown reception |
| 3 | **Posture alignment** | 0.15 | actively welcomes no-decipherment / methodology / negative results | expects positive "solutions" |
| 4 | **Cost** | 0.10 | diamond / free | high APC, no waiver |
| 5 | **Speed / window fit** | 0.15 | fast turnaround or an in-window deadline we can hit | slow / no near window |
| 6 | **Reach to target audience** | 0.10 | flagship for the right readers | niche / low visibility |
| 7 | **Feasibility / low adaptation** | 0.10 | fits our format as-is | heavy reframing / hard bar |

Posture alignment (#3) is deliberately weighted: our L7 "no decipherment" stance is an asset —
venues that reject "solutions" are *safer*, not riskier, for us.

## Reading the ranking

- **Paper A** is spoilt for good homes. The two ACL journals (**TACL**, **CL**) top the list:
  free, flagship, undeciphered-script appetite, and they *reject* solution claims — which we
  don't make. **HistoCrypt** is the best *topical* fit but its deadline is unconfirmed (winter).
  **DHQ** is the strongest for a *humanities* audience and has a direct 2026 Voynich precedent.
- **Paper B**'s best economics are **LRE** (free non-OA route, evaluation-methodology home).
  **EACL via ARR** gives top NLP reach but a higher main-track bar and an imminent (Aug 3)
  deadline. **Cambridge NLP** fits best on scope but the £2,610 APC drags it down absent a waiver.
- **Speed vs quality tension:** the fastest-to-visibility options (CHR Aug 14, EACL via ARR
  Aug 3) score lower on precedent/reach but higher on speed. If getting *in front of the
  community soon* matters more than journal prestige, they lead; if not, the journals win.

## How to use this against a specific target

For any concrete decision, re-score the row with the *current* fact (a confirmed deadline
bumps #5; a granted APC waiver bumps #4). The pathway briefs (`pathways/*/v1.md`) carry the
per-target scores that drove each pathway's venue choice.
