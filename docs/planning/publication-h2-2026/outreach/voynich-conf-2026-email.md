**Subject:** Late enquiry — a reproducible, no-decipherment computational study of MS 408

Dear [ORGANIZER NAME / Voynich 2026 Programme Committee],

I'm writing to ask, with apologies for the late timing, whether there might be any
possibility of a poster, short-talk, or discussant slot at the Voynich Manuscript Conference

I lead an open, reproducible computational program on MS 408 whose explicit stance is *not*
to propose a reading. It is a firewall-disciplined statistical characterization that aims to
*shrink* the space of viable hypotheses rather than announce a solution: we confirm the
low-entropy anomaly and the two-system (Currier A/B) structure, show that no single statistic
localizes meaning, and use a mid-level syntactic signature to exclude word-order-preserving
ciphers of real prose while finding the verbose-homophonic class inconclusive. Every claim
carries an explicit A–D evidence grade, and every number is produced by deterministic,
versioned code — so the work is fully reproducible and makes no decipherment claim.

Given the conference's stated position against "solution" claims, I hope this framing is a
natural fit rather than another purported decode. The work is available as a preprint
