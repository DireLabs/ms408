## Tailored framing (title + abstract)
**Working title:** *A Firewall-Disciplined Statistical Characterization of the Voynich
Manuscript: Reproducible Discriminators that Shrink the Hypothesis Space.*

**Abstract (v1, TACL-tuned — empirical/contribution-first):** We present a reproducible,
firewall-disciplined statistical characterization of Beinecke MS 408 (the Voynich Manuscript)
and release it as an open tool. Rather than propose a reading, we build a battery of
substitution-invariant discriminators — conditional character entropy, Montemurro–Zanette
word-order information, edit-distance-1 morphology, Zipf slope, and null-corrected mid-level
syntax measures — and use them to *exclude* hypothesis classes. We confirm the low-entropy
anomaly and a two-system (Currier A/B) structure, show that no single statistic localizes
meaning (structured-meaningless generators match or exceed the manuscript on every axis),
robustly exclude word-order-preserving ciphers of real prose, and find the verbose+homophonic
class inconclusive. Every claim is evidence-graded; all numbers are produced by deterministic,
versioned code writing machine-readable results. We make no decipherment claim.
