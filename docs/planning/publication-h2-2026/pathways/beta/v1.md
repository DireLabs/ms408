The natural home for **evaluation methodology** for ground-truth-free corpora. Hybrid OA with a

## Tailored framing (title + abstract)
**Working title:** *Adversarial Self-Correction for Ground-Truth-Free Corpora: A Harness,
Firewall, and Clean-Context Refutation Protocol, with an Undeciphered-Manuscript Case Study.*

**Abstract (v1, LRE-tuned — methodology/evaluation-first):** Computational study of corpora with
no ground truth is unusually prone to plausible-but-wrong conclusions. We describe an operating
architecture that couples three disciplines: a synthetic ground-truth **harness** (every method
must separate known positives from matched null/meaningless controls before touching the real
object); a **firewall** (every reported number is produced by deterministic, versioned code that
records its script, commit, inputs, and parameters); and a standing **clean-context adversarial
refutation pass** whose briefs are archived. Over an eleven-iteration program on the Voynich
Manuscript, we report a firewall-verified record in which over a dozen first-pass conclusions —
several asserted by the program's own analysis code, two of them negatives — were narrowed,
withdrawn, or retracted. We release the harness, controls, and evaluation tooling, and offer the
architecture with an adoption checklist as a disciplined default for reproducible,
over-claim-resistant research on undeciphered or otherwise unverifiable corpora.
