**The time-boxed pathway.** Its whole value is *imminent, confirmed deadlines* → fastest path to
community visibility. If we don't act on the August slots, delta degrades to "monitor the winter
workshop cluster," and the journals (alpha/beta/gamma) carry the program.

## Recommended delta play
- If we want an August push: **CHR 2027 (Aug 14) with Paper A** is the higher-probability,
  lower-stress of the two imminent slots (CEUR, humanities-data framing, 3 weeks). Treat **EACL
  via ARR (Aug 3)** as a stretch only if Paper B is truly ready this week.
- Regardless: **arXiv-post v7 + methods-v3 now** (allowed; no ACL blackout) to timestamp
  priority ahead of any conference submission, and to give alpha/beta/gamma a citable preprint.
- Put **HistoCrypt 2027** on a watch list for the winter cluster — it's the best-fit conference
  and worth targeting even if we skip August.

## Risks
The August deadlines are genuinely tight; a rushed submission risks a weaker first impression at
venues we could hit cleanly later. CEUR/workshop prestige < journals. The 2027 CFP dates for the
best-fit venues (HistoCrypt, SIGTYP, LaTeCH) are unconfirmed — don't build the plan on them
firming up on schedule.
