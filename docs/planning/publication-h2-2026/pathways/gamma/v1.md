## Tailored framing (title + abstract)
**Working title:** *What the Statistics Can and Cannot Say About the Voynich Manuscript: A
Firewall-Disciplined, Evidence-Graded Constraint Study.*

**Abstract (v1, DHQ-tuned — humanistic argument-first):** The Voynich Manuscript has drawn a
century of confident, mutually incompatible "solutions," a pattern enabled by the absence of any
ground truth against which a reading can be checked. We take the opposite stance: rather than
claim what the book says, we ask, for each interpretable dimension, *how far along it sits* — and
we make every such claim carry an explicit evidence grade produced by deterministic, reproducible
code. We confirm the manuscript's low character-entropy anomaly and its two-system (Currier A/B)
structure, show why purely distributional statistics cannot localize meaning (structured-
meaningless processes reproduce them), and turn a mid-level syntactic signature into a
cryptanalytic discriminator that robustly excludes word-order-preserving ciphers of real prose
while leaving verbose-homophonic ciphers inconclusive. The contribution is a disciplined way to
*shrink the space of viable hypotheses* about an undeciphered artifact — and an honest account of
where distributional methods stop.
