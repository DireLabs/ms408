The ΔI collapse E29 rests on is **not an informative refutation of the cipher hypothesis** —
it is largely pre-ordained by (a) Greshko's *respacing* and (b) *homophony per se*, both of

1. **The ΔI collapse happens in the PLAINTEXT, before encryption.** Word-boundary Pliny Latin

   stated mechanism ("homophone draws PLUS respacing decouple…") mis-attributes it: the
   word-boundary Latin is already in the VMS band, and respacing — a spacing convention, not a

2. **ΔI collapses under homophony alone, word order + boundaries fixed.** H-sweep on

   H=18 → 0.009; H=32 → 0.005. Word order is identical across rows. So ΔI is a homophony /
   type-token-coupling detector, **not** a clean "retained word-order information" measure —
   which disqualifies E29's "carried by the ROBUST ΔI axis, not the soft measures."
3. **Not like-for-like.** The VMS's own retained ΔI is BLOCK/section structure (our i06/E1/E2),

4. **"CONFIRMED" is selective.** E29 matches only 1/8 of its own bands and measures NONE of the
   glyph-level / prefix-suffix / word-length-distribution properties Greshko actually claims to
