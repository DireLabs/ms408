\begin{abstract}
\noindent We present a reproducible, firewall-disciplined statistical characterization of
Beinecke MS~408 (the Voynich Manuscript) and release it as an open evaluation tool. Rather
than propose a reading, we build a battery of substitution-invariant discriminators ---
conditional character entropy, Montemurro--Zanette word-order information, edit-distance-1
morphology, Zipf slope, and null-corrected mid-level syntax measures --- and use them to
\emph{exclude} classes of hypothesis. We confirm the low-entropy anomaly ($h_2\approx 2.08$ vs
$3.07$--$3.91$ for medieval natural-language controls) and a genuine two-system (Currier~A/B)
structure; we show that \emph{no single statistic, nor a vector of them, localizes meaning}
(against matched structured-meaningless baselines, a meaningless generator out-scores
meaningful text on every axis); and, turning a mid-level syntactic signature into a
cryptanalytic discriminator, we robustly exclude word-order-preserving ciphers of real prose
($6.8$--$8.0\sigma$ once the null is deconfounded from topic drift) while finding the
verbose-homophonic class inconclusive. Every claim carries an explicit A--D evidence grade;
every number is produced by deterministic, versioned code writing machine-readable results, and
the discriminator battery is released so any hypothesis can be scored against the manuscript's
reference bands. We make no decipherment claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The Voynich Manuscript is an early-fifteenth-century codex \citep{dimperio1978elegant} in an
undeciphered script, and a magnet for confident, mutually incompatible ``solutions.'' The
structural reason is simple: with no ground truth against which to check a reading, ``it comes
out looking like language / a cipher / a number system'' is nearly unfalsifiable, and the
analyst's degrees of freedom are vast. We take the opposite stance. Rather than announce what
the text says, we ask, for each measurable dimension, \emph{how far along it sits}, and we
\emph{shrink} the space of hypotheses consistent with the evidence.

Concretely, we contribute:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item a battery of substitution-invariant discriminators for an undeciphered word-stream
  corpus, each computed against a matched null, and released as an open tool that scores an
  arbitrary token stream against the manuscript's reference bands (\S\ref{sec:method},
  \S\ref{sec:tool});
  \item a demonstration that these statistics \emph{cannot localize meaning}: matched
  structured-meaningless processes reproduce or exceed the manuscript on every axis
  (\S\ref{sec:meaning});
  \item a cryptanalytic result that \emph{robustly excludes word-order-preserving ciphers} of
  real prose on a mid-level syntactic signature, while showing --- against a
  concurrently-published verbose-homophonic cipher --- that the word-order axis it was thought
  to rest on is confounded, leaving the homophonic class inconclusive (\S\ref{sec:cipher});
  \item all under a firewall (every number from versioned code) and evidence grading, so the
  characterization is reproducible and honestly bounded (\S\ref{sec:method}).
\end{itemize}
The goal is a smaller, better-defended envelope, not a decode.

\section{Related work}
Computational study of the manuscript has a long lineage: \citet{currier1976} identified the
two ``languages'' A and B; \citet{reddy2011know} surveyed its statistical properties for a
computational-linguistics audience; \citet{montemurro2013keywords} measured long-range
word-order information; \citet{bowern2021linguistics} synthesized the linguistic evidence; and
\citet{lindemann2022entropy} characterized its anomalous conditional character entropy.
Decipherment \emph{methods} for lost scripts have matured in CL --- e.g.\ minimum-cost-flow and
phonetic-prior models for Ugaritic, Linear~B, and undersegmented scripts
\citep{luo2019neural,luo2021deciphering} --- but these assume a recoverable target language,
which for MS~408 is exactly what is in doubt. Our contribution is orthogonal: not a decoder but
a set of \emph{discriminators} that constrain which such efforts could even be well-posed. We
engage two concurrent cipher accounts \citep{greshko2025naibbe,parisel2026layered} directly in
\S\ref{sec:cipher}.

\section{Data and transliteration}
We use the manuscript text in the Zandbergen--Landini (ZL) EVA transliteration
\citep{zandbergen_voynichnu}, parsed from IVTFF, and stratify every text analysis by Currier
system (A/B) and, where relevant, scribal hand \citep{fagindavis2020hands}. Our measures are
\emph{substitution-invariant} (unchanged under renaming glyphs), so no arbitrary phonetic
rendering is assumed; we additionally run a v101-transliteration sensitivity pass. About
$12\%$ of the foliated range is lost and the vocabulary is non-saturating, which bounds every
lexical statistic from below (\S\ref{sec:limits}).

\section{Method: discriminators, harness, firewall}
\label{sec:method}
\textbf{The discriminator battery.} For a token stream we compute: conditional character
entropy $h_2$ (Lindemann--Bowern, spaces included); the Montemurro--Zanette word-order
information $\Delta I$ and its peak scale; edit-distance-1 (ED1) morphology-network main-component
share; the Zipf rank--frequency slope; the type--token ratio; and two null-corrected
\emph{mid-level} syntax measures --- a function/content collocation gap and an adjacent
word-class NMI \citep{brown1992class} --- each expressed as a $z$-score against an ensemble of
order-shuffle nulls that share the corpus's own nuisance parameters, so the $z$ is
nuisance-controlled by construction and comparable across corpora.

\textbf{Harness and matched controls.} No measure is trusted on the manuscript until it
separates known positives (real language, several typologically diverse) from matched
\emph{structured-meaningless} controls (self-citation/auto-copying \citep{timm2020selfcitation},
positional/template and reuse generators) and from ciphers of real prose (verbose, abjad,
nomenclator, and verbose-homophonic). This ``harness-first'' rule is what lets a negative
result carry weight.

\textbf{Firewall and grading.} Every reported number is produced by deterministic, versioned
code writing machine-readable results (fixed seeds; each result records its script, commit,
input, and parameters); nothing is estimated or recalled. Every claim carries an A--D evidence
grade. Strong claims additionally pass a standing clean-context adversarial-refutation step
before they stand; that protocol is the subject of a companion paper and is summarized here only
where it changed a result.

\section{Results}
\subsection{A low-entropy, two-system script \grade{A/B}}
The manuscript's conditional character entropy is anomalously low, $h_2\approx 2.08$, versus
$3.07$--$3.91$ for medieval natural-language controls --- a robust, substitution-invariant
anomaly. Currier~A and B are genuinely distinct systems, and the two are separable on
co-occurrence structure. The morphology is dense and paradigmatic: the ED1 main component
covers $\approx 0.80$ of word types (a $10$k-token subsample gives $\approx 0.75$).

\subsection{No statistic localizes meaning \grade{C}}
\label{sec:meaning}
A central negative: across the battery, \emph{no axis --- nor a vector of them --- separates
meaningful from meaningless text} in a way the manuscript satisfies. Against honest
structured-meaningless baselines, a meaningless generator out-scores meaningful text on every
axis we can measure, and the manuscript sits at or below both. This is why we reframe the
question from ``what does it say'' to ``how far along each dimension does it sit,'' and it marks
the boundary at which purely distributional methods stop.

\subsection{Ciphers of real prose: order-preserving excluded, homophonic inconclusive}
\label{sec:cipher}
The mid-level syntax measures double as a cryptanalytic discriminator, because a
word-order-\emph{preserving} cipher of a real language inherits the source's strong surface
syntax, whereas the manuscript's is weak. A $1{:}1$ substitution, a vowel-dropping abjad, and a
light nomenclator applied to Latin all show strong function/content and word-class $z$
(e.g.\ $13.8$/$21.0$/$9.8$ and $11.3$/$13.0$), versus the manuscript's weak
$z\approx-1.2$/$1.9$ (A), $-4.7$/$2.6$ (B). The gap is \emph{language-universal} --- no natural
control is weak, including consonantal Hebrew, a native abjad --- and once the null is
deconfounded from section/topic vocabulary drift (a within-block shuffle), an order-preserving
cipher sits $6.8\sigma$ (function/content) and $8.0\sigma$ (word-class) from the manuscript.
\textbf{So word-order-preserving ciphers of real prose are robustly excluded \grade{B}.}

We initially over-reached to the \emph{entire} cipher-of-real-prose class via a second leg ---
that ciphers destroy the manuscript's retained word-order information $\Delta I$. Engaging a
concurrent, hand-constructed verbose-homophonic cipher \citep{greshko2025naibbe} forced a
retraction: its apparent $\Delta I$ collapse is an artifact of \emph{respacing} (word-boundary
Latin already has $\Delta I=0.076$, inside the manuscript's band; $\approx\!82\%$ of the loss
precedes the cipher) and of \emph{homophony} (with word order fixed, $\Delta I$ falls
monotonically as homophones-per-type rises). $\Delta I$ is therefore a
homophony/type-token-coupling detector, not a clean word-order axis. A block-scale, like-for-like
$\Delta I$ analysis on the $(h_2,\Delta I)$ plane \emph{weakly} separates verbose-homophonic
ciphers but does not revive $\Delta I$ into a hard discriminator. \textbf{We therefore report the
verbose-homophonic (Naibbe-class) case as inconclusive \grade{C}} --- neither robustly excluded
nor robustly reached --- converging with \citet{greshko2025naibbe} and adjacent to the
cipher-like reading of \citet{parisel2026layered}. No decipherment or meaning claim is made or
implied; this is a statement about which cipher classes reproduce the distributional signature.

\subsection{The discriminator battery as a released tool}
\label{sec:tool}
The battery is released as an open package (withheld for review) with a single entry point that
scores an arbitrary token stream against the manuscript's reference bands and returns, per axis,
the value, the band, whether it lands inside, and \emph{the caveat that axis carries} --- the
confounded $\Delta I$, the token-count-sensitive type--token ratio, and the soft mid-level
measures are flagged and not counted, so a verdict cannot be quoted without its hedges. The
reference bands are themselves built by versioned code (subsample-without-replacement CIs) and
ship as a checked artifact; a verification path reproduces them from source. We intend the tool
as a reusable evaluation resource for undeciphered-corpus work: a hypothesis (a cipher output, a
generator, a transliteration variant) is \emph{necessary}-tested, not confirmed --- matching the
bands means a hypothesis is not excluded, never that it is the mechanism.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
The evidence is a single manuscript-length corpus, so several results are power-limited; the
text is incomplete ($\approx\!12\%$ lost, non-saturating vocabulary), bounding lexical
statistics from below; the two soft mid-level $z$-measures have a manuscript-side subsample CI
that crosses zero (we do not count them and report the gap that drives the cipher exclusion
either way); $\Delta I$ is confounded as above; and results are primarily EVA-based with v101 as
a sensitivity layer, not a full replication. Grades reflect these limits.

\section{Conclusion}
Treating an undeciphered corpus as an object to \emph{constrain} rather than \emph{solve}
yields reproducible, falsifiable results: a low-entropy two-system script whose meaning is not
localizable by any statistic we have, that is not a word-order-preserving cipher of real prose,
and for which a verbose-homophonic cipher and a generative account both remain live. The
transferable artifact is the firewall-disciplined discriminator battery, released so others can
test their own hypotheses against the same bands.

\section*{Reproducibility}
All statistics are produced by deterministic, versioned code writing machine-readable results;
the discriminator battery, the reference-band artifact, a verification path, and the archived
adversarial-refutation briefs accompany the paper (repository withheld for double-blind review).
